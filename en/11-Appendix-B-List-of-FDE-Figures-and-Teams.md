# Appendix B List of FDE Figures and Teams

This appendix lists the figures, teams, and knowledge sources that are unavoidable when understanding the FDE model. For a complete list of sources referenced during the writing of this book, see the research notes published along with the manuscript.

## 1. Key Figures

**Shyam Sankar** — President and Chief Technology Officer of Palantir, early employee. Widely considered the inventor of the FDE strategy: the person who redefined "on-site customization" from a cost to "product discovery". His public speeches are firsthand material for understanding Palantir's philosophy.

**Bob McGrew** — Early PayPal engineer, early Palantir executive, former Chief Research Officer at OpenAI (led the R&D of ChatGPT, GPT-4, and o1). The best interpreter of the FDE model. "Gravel roads and highways" and "Do things that don't scale, at scale" both come from him. Must-read: YC Lightcone podcast "The FDE Playbook for AI Startups" (September 2025).

**Stephen Cohen** — Co-founder of Palantir. Inventor of the demo loop: the protagonist of "This thing is terrible" and "Then how would you like it to be different?".

**Alex Karp** — Chief Executive Officer of Palantir. Proposer of the "French waiter" behavior model: opposing a submissive, order-taking engineering culture.

**Barry** — Former Forward Deployed Engineer at Palantir, author of the article "Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering". The most important "insider warning" on the FDE model: the true ledger of costs, chaos, and burnout.

**Ted Mabrey / Nabeel Qureshi** — Palantir-affiliated writers who have published in-depth public writings on how Palantir operates.

**Colin Jarvis** — Head of the Forward Deployed Engineering team at OpenAI, the person who built OpenAI's FDE structure from scratch.

**Brad Lightcap** — Chief Operating Officer of OpenAI, the leader behind the "deployment company".

**Natalie Meurer** — Head of Agent Engineering at Sierra (formerly five years at Palantir). The person who coined "Agent Engineer". Must-read: Latent Space podcast interview (July 2026).

**Bret Taylor / Clay Bavor** — Founders of Sierra (the former was Co-CEO of Salesforce, the latter was Vice President at Google). The people who turned the FDE model into the business model itself (outcome-based pricing).

**Jesse Zhang / Ashwin Sreenivas** — Founders of Decagon (the latter is from Palantir). Designers of the agent operation process, the template for "productizing delivery experience".

**David Wakeling** — Former Head of Markets Innovation at Linklaters, the internal champion for Harvey's first lighthouse deployment. The best example from the perspective of a client-side champion.

## 2. Iconic Teams

**Palantir FDE Organization** — The inventor and complete form of the model. The "Echo-Delta-Platform" triangle, the bootcamp machine.

**OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineering Team** — Formed in 2024, globally distributed, the operators behind John Deere and BBVA deployments; upgraded to a "deployment company" in 2026.

**Anthropic Applied AI Team** — The FDE structure formed under the name of "Applied AI Engineers", co-builders of the FIS financial crime agent; formed an enterprise AI services joint venture with Blackstone and others in 2026.

**Sierra Agent Engineering Team** — The ultimate form of outcome-based pricing + managed delivery (founded in 2023, valued at $4.5 billion in October 2024, and reportedly continued to climb since then).

**Harvey Deployment Team** — The textbook for vertical industry (legal) FDE (founded in 2022, valued at $11 billion in March 2026, user coverage of 100,000+ lawyers).

**Scale AI / Databricks / Salesforce / Google Cloud / Mistral / Cohere** — Each has FDE or isomorphic roles, with different names (Solutions Architect, Customer Engineer, etc.), but the exact same framework.

**Volcengine Doubao Large Model FDE Team** — The most explicit FDE structure among major Chinese internet companies (established in 2026, covering automotive, healthcare, education, finance, and semiconductors; co-building a thousand-person team with EY).

**Chinese Domestic FDE Pioneers** — Domestic service providers based in real estate facility management and the financial industry, who have publicly output the defining thought that "FDE does not equal on-site outsourcing".
