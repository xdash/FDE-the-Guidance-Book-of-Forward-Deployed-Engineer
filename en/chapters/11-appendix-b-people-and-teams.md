# Appendix B — FDE People and Teams

This appendix lists the people, teams, and knowledge sources you can't get around in understanding the FDE model. For the complete list of sources referenced while writing this book, see the research notes released alongside the manuscript.

## I. Key People

**Shyam Sankar** — President and CTO of Palantir, an early employee. Widely regarded as the inventor of the FDE strategy: the one who redefined "on-site customization" from cost to "product discovery." His public remarks are firsthand material for understanding Palantir's philosophy.

**Bob McGrew** — early PayPal engineer, early Palantir executive, former Chief Research Officer at OpenAI (led development of ChatGPT, GPT-4, o1). The best interpreter of the FDE model. "Gravel roads and highways" and "doing things that don't scale, at scale" both came from him. Essential: the YC Lightcone podcast, *The FDE Playbook for AI Startups* (September 2025).

**Stephen Cohen** — Palantir co-founder. Inventor of the demo loop: the protagonist of "this thing is terrible" and "then how would you want it to be different."

**Alex Karp** — CEO of Palantir. Originator of the "French waiter" behavioral model: opposed to a servile, order-taking engineering culture.

**Barry** — former Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer, author of the article *Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering*. The most important "insider's warning" of the FDE model: the real ledger of cost, chaos, and burnout.

**Ted Mabrey / Nabeel Qureshi** — Palantir-lineage writers with deep public writing on how Palantir operates.

**Colin Jarvis** — head of OpenAI's forward-deployment engineering team, the one who built OpenAI's FDE organization from scratch.

**Brad Lightcap** — COO of OpenAI, the driver of the "deployment company."

**Natalie Meurer** — head of agent engineering at Sierra (five years at Palantir). Coiner of "Agent Engineer." Essential: the Latent Space podcast interview (July 2026).

**Bret Taylor / Clay Bavor** — Sierra founders (the former was co-CEO of Salesforce, the latter a VP at Google). The ones who turned the FDE model into the business model itself (outcome-based pricing).

**Jesse Zhang / Ashwin Sreenivas** — Decagon founders (the latter came out of Palantir). Designers of agent operating flows, the model of "productizing delivery experience."

**David Wakeling** — former head of market innovation at Linklaters, the in-firm champion of Harvey's first lighthouse deployment. The best sample of the customer-side champion's perspective.

## II. Landmark Teams

**Palantir FDE organization** — the model's inventor and finished form. The "Echo-Delta-Platform" triangle, the bootcamp machine.

**OpenAI forward-deployment engineering team** — formed in 2024, globally distributed, the operators of John Deere and BBVA; upgraded to the "deployment company" in 2026.

**Anthropic applied-AI team** — an FDE organization formed under the name "Applied AI Engineer," co-builder of the FIS financial-crime agent; formed an enterprise-AI-services joint venture with Blackstone and others in 2026.

**Sierra agent-engineering team** — the extreme form of outcome-based pricing + managed delivery (founded 2023, valued at $4.5 billion in October 2024 and reportedly climbing since).

**Harvey deployment team** — the textbook of the vertical-industry (legal) FDE (founded 2022, valued at $11 billion in March 2026, users covering 100,000+ lawyers).

**Scale AI / Databricks / Salesforce / Google Cloud / Mistral / Cohere** — each has an FDE or isomorphic role under a different name (solution architect, customer engineer, etc.), with the same skeleton.

**Volcengine Doubao large-model FDE team** — the most explicit FDE organization among China's internet giants (formed 2026, covering automotive, healthcare, education, finance, semiconductors; co-building a thousand-strong team with EY).

**China's local FDE early movers** — local service providers with real-estate/facilities-management and finance as their base, who publicly output the defining reflection that "the FDE ≠ staff-augmentation outsourcing."

## III. Knowledge Sources (ranked by importance)

1. YC Lightcone podcast: *The FDE Playbook for AI Startups — Interview with Bob McGrew* (September 2025) — a 51-minute retrospective by the model's first narrator.
2. a16z: *Trading Margin for Moat* — the benchmark of business-model analysis.
3. Barry: *Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering* — an insider's cool-headed warning.
4. MIT NANDA lab: *The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025* — the source of the "95% failure rate," the data bedrock for the FDE's reason to exist.
5. The New Stack: *Why OpenAI and Anthropic Are Both Racing to Build FDE Teams* (May 2026).
6. CIO.com: *Anthropic's Financial Agent Exposes a New Bottleneck: the Forward Deployed Engineer* (May 2026) — the buyer's perspective and Gartner's warning.
7. Latent Space podcast: *The Forward Deployed Engineer and the Future of Software Engineering* (July 2026) — Sierra's perspective.
8. Palantir official blog: *A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer* (2020) — an official self-account, with a Chinese translation in circulation.
9. The OpenFDE community (open-fde.com) — the practitioners' open-source community, a map of roles and companies.
10. 36Kr's *China's B2B Software: Escaping the Trap of Loss-Making Growth*, and the FDE series of articles on Chen Guo (George)'s public account — essential Chinese-context comparisons.

- GetPerspective: *2026 Forward Deployed Engineer Compensation Report* (1,200 samples) — a mid-level FDE at a top lab has a median total annual compensation of about $385,000, a senior about $610,000, and a principal over $1.2 million.
- fdenest.com / fde.academy / sundeepteki.org — three dedicated sites on interview preparation and career paths.
- Levels.fyi — real-time compensation data for roles at Palantir and others.
- The job postings themselves are the best textbook: the FDE job postings of OpenAI, Anthropic, Decagon, Harvey, and Scale AI are worth studying word for word.
