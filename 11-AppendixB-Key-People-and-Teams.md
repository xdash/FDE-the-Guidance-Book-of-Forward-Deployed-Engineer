# Appendix B: Key People and Teams in FDE

This appendix lists the people, teams, and knowledge sources one cannot avoid when trying to understand the FDE model. The complete list of sources referenced during the writing of this book is available in the research notes published alongside the manuscript.

## I. Key People

**Shyam Sankar** — President and Chief Technology Officer of Palantir, an early employee. Widely regarded as the inventor of the FDE strategy: the person who redefined "on-site customization" from a cost center into "product discovery." His public remarks are the primary source for understanding Palantir's philosophy.

**Bob McGrew** — Early PayPal engineer, early Palantir executive, former Chief Research Officer at OpenAI (led development of ChatGPT, GPT-4, and o1). The best interpreter of the FDE model. Phrases like "gravel roads and highways" and "doing things that don't scale, at scale" both originate with him. Essential listening: the YC Lightcone podcast episode "The FDE Playbook for AI Startups" (September 2025).

**Stephen Cohen** — Co-founder of Palantir. Inventor of the demo loop: the protagonist of "this thing sucks" / "then what do you want it to look like instead."

**Alex Karp** — CEO of Palantir. Originator of the "French waiter" behavioral model: opposed to a servile, order-taking engineering culture.

**Barry** — Former Palantir forward deployed engineer, author of "Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering." The single most important "insider warning" on the FDE model: a candid ledger of its costs, chaos, and burnout.

**Ted Mabrey / Nabeel Qureshi** — Writers within the Palantir orbit, who have produced in-depth public writing on how Palantir operates.

**Colin Jarvis** — Head of OpenAI's forward deployed engineering team, the person who built OpenAI's FDE function from scratch.

**Brad Lightcap** — Chief Operating Officer of OpenAI, the driving force behind the "Deployment Company."

**Natalie Meurer** — Head of Agent Engineering at Sierra (five years at Palantir prior). The person who coined the term "agent engineer." Essential listening: the Latent Space podcast interview (July 2026).

**Bret Taylor / Clay Bavor** — Co-founders of Sierra (the former previously served as co-CEO of Salesforce, the latter as a Vice President at Google). The people who turned the FDE model into a business model in its own right (outcome-based pricing).

**Jesse Zhang / Ashwin Sreenivas** — Co-founders of Decagon (the latter came from Palantir). Designers of the agent operating workflow, an exemplar of "productizing delivery experience."

**David Wakeling** — Former Head of Market Innovation at Linklaters, and the in-house champion of Harvey's first lighthouse deployment. The best example of the client-side champion's perspective.

## II. Landmark Teams

**Palantir's FDE organization** — The inventor of the model and its most fully realized form. The "Echo-Delta-Platform" triangle, and the bootcamp machine.

**OpenAI's forward deployed engineering team** — Formed in 2024, globally distributed, the operator behind the John Deere and BBVA engagements; upgraded into the "Deployment Company" in 2026.

**Anthropic's Applied AI team** — An FDE-equivalent function organized under the name "Applied AI Engineer," co-builder of the FIS financial crime agent; formed a joint enterprise-AI-services venture with Blackstone and others in 2026.

**Sierra's Agent Engineering team** — The extreme form of outcome-based pricing combined with managed delivery (founded 2023, valued at $4.5 billion as of October 2024, and reportedly continuing to climb since).

**Harvey's deployment team** — The textbook case of vertical-industry (legal) FDE (founded 2022, valued at $11 billion as of March 2026, with coverage across 100,000+ lawyers).

**Scale AI / Databricks / Salesforce / Google Cloud / Mistral / Cohere** — Each maintains FDE-equivalent roles under different names (solutions architect, customer engineer, etc.), but with an identical underlying structure.

**Volcano Engine Doubao Large Model FDE team** — The clearest FDE function among China's major internet companies (established 2026, covering automotive, healthcare, education, finance, and semiconductors; co-building a thousand-person-scale team with EY).

**China's homegrown FDE pioneers** — Domestic service providers operating in real-estate facility management and financial services, who have publicly articulated the defining thought that "FDE does not equal on-site staff augmentation."

## III. Knowledge Sources (in order of importance)

1. YC Lightcone podcast: "The FDE Playbook for AI Startups—an interview with Bob McGrew" (September 2025)—a 51-minute retrospective from the model's first narrator.
2. a16z: "Trading Margin for Moat"—the benchmark analysis of the business model.
3. Barry: "Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering"—a sober warning from an insider.
4. MIT NANDA Lab: "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025"—the source of the "95% failure rate" figure, and the data foundation for the FDE model's raison d'être.
5. The New Stack: "Why OpenAI and Anthropic Are Both Racing to Build FDE Teams" (May 2026).
6. CIO.com: "Anthropic's Financial Agents Expose the New Bottleneck: Forward Deployed Engineers" (May 2026)—a buyer's-side perspective and the Gartner warning.
7. Latent Space podcast: "Forward Deployed Engineers and the Future of Software Engineering" (July 2026)—Sierra's perspective.
8. Palantir's official blog: "A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer" (2020)—an official first-person account, with a Chinese translation in circulation.
9. The OpenFDE community (open-fde.com)—a practitioner-driven open community mapping roles and companies.
10. 36Kr's "Chinese To-B Software: Escaping the Trap of Loss-Making Growth," and George Chen's WeChat public-account FDE series—essential counterparts for the Chinese context.

## IV. Compensation and Job-Search References

- GetPerspective: "2026 Forward Deployed Engineer Compensation Report" (1,200-person sample)—median total compensation among leading labs is roughly $385,000 for mid-level FDEs, roughly $610,000 for senior FDEs, and over $1.2 million for principal-level FDEs.
- fdenest.com / fde.academy / sundeepteki.org — three specialized sites for interview preparation and career pathing.
- Levels.fyi — real-time compensation data for roles at Palantir and others.
- Job postings themselves are the best textbook: the FDE job listings from OpenAI, Anthropic, Decagon, Harvey, and Scale AI are worth studying word for word.
